I design and engineer at the intersection of form and function. Currently Product Engineer at Tajji, building a property platform for Africa, end to end.

work@victorkambi.com Get in touch → More about me →

My path into product started in graphic design, in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign. Computer science at Moi University gave me the other half, and I’ve been working between the two ever since.

In 2022 I joined Emmerce as a frontend engineering intern, designing and building responsive websites and digital campaigns for consumer brands. It taught me to ship for real audiences, on real deadlines.

In 2023 I joined Tajji Real Estate as a founding engineer. I set up the initial architecture and shipped the first versions of the platform, laying down both the engineering and the design foundations.

Since 2024 I’ve been Tajji’s product engineer: the backend, the design system and the product experience. That covers BomaOS for landlords and managers, Jirani for tenants, the BomaOS Console on the web, and Sceptre, the system that holds them together.

I like the mechanical parts to have feeling. Interaction should be enjoyable without costing performance, and the systems underneath should be as carefully designed as the screens on top.

BomaOS

Android · iOS · Desktop · 2025

Property operations for landlords and managers: portfolios, units, leases, collections and settlement, from one Kotlin codebase.

Google Play →

  1. Collections first. The month’s money is the first thing you see, against what’s due.
  2. Two taps to record. Amount, method, reference. M-Pesa is the default because it’s how Kenya pays.
  3. The receipt is the ledger. Every payment posts balanced entries before it becomes a number.

Jirani

Tenant app · 2026

The other side of every lease. Tenants claim an invitation, verify who they are, and pay rent where they already keep their money.

  1. The amount, then the breakdown. Rent and service charge are itemised, never hidden.
  2. A deliberate gesture. Swipe to pay, so money never moves on an accidental tap.

BomaOS Console

Web · Next.js · 2026

Where a portfolio is commissioned: sign the agreement, pick a plan, pay, and you’re live. It also runs billing, invoices and team access.

Sceptre

Compose Multiplatform · design system

One set of tokens, typography, components and motion behind BomaOS, Jirani and the shared libraries, so a change lands in both apps in the same commit.

Identity verification

KYC · KYB · multiplatform

Verifying people and organisations before money or keys change hands, as a library both apps share.

  1. One step at a time. ID, then selfie, with progress always visible.
  2. Guides, not instructions. The frame shows where the card goes.
